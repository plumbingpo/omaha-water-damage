Home / Services / Burst Pipe Repair

Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Omaha Restoration Co provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Omaha and surrounding areas. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Everyone in the house should be able to locate and turn the main water valve in a hurry
  • Unheated spaces like garages, attics and crawl spaces need pipe insulation on exposed lines
  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early

When to Call for Burst Pipe Repair in Omaha

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A sudden drop in water pressure throughout your property
  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside
  • Rusty or discolored water coming from your taps
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Wet spots, bulging or staining on walls or ceilings
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak

Why Burst Pipe Repair Is Not a DIY Job

Burst pipe water damage often extends far beyond what is visible on the surface. It moves between ceiling joists, inside floor assemblies, beside electrical conduit and down wall cavities, soaking materials you might not find for days or even weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Our technicians find all the wet areas at the start by tracing migration with thermal imaging and meter readings.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.
